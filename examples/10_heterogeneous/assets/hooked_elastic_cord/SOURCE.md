# Hooked elastic cord

A 250 mm × Ø4 mm silicone cord has 10 mm tapers, 24 × 12 × 8 mm ABS grips and Ø6 mm hooks with 8 mm nominal inner radii.

OpenCASCADE Boolean solids share triangulated material interfaces. TetGen filled the volume, Netgen optimized its interior, and final tetrahedron centroids selected part labels. The mesh contains 5,884 vertices and 20,049 tetrahedra. Parts 0 and 2 are ABS hooks and grips (2.2 GPa). Part 1 is the silicone cord and tapers (1 MPa). Both mouths face −Y. The bundle underwent static checks only.
